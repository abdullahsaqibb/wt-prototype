# World Treasures identity

Approved name: **World Treasures**, with a final S.

Approved logo: open carton with a snack packet, drink can and route detail, paired with a stacked wordmark. Standalone copies are in `assets/brand/`; these are raster concept assets, not editable vector masters.

| Colour | Hex | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deep aubergine | `#281D35` | Main text, buttons and dark sections |
| Warm ivory | `#F7F3EB` | Main background |
| Burnt orange | `#D94B26` | Emphasis and highlights |
| Pistachio | `#D5E890` | Exclusive banner and selected accents |
| Soft lavender | `#DCD3EB` | Product showcase and supporting sections |

Headings: **Bricolage Grotesque**, weights 500–800.

Body, navigation and checkout: **DM Sans**, weights 400–700.

Both are loaded from Google Fonts with local fallback stacks. Keep text and buttons high contrast; bright accents are primarily surfaces or decoration. Carousel scenes use coordinated tints without recolouring photographed packaging.
